HOW TO USE
----------
1. Open a FRESH session with your implementer model (the cheaper one).
2. Only hand off a TSPEC that is READY:
     - all its `depends_on` are COMPLETE (see eos/state/project.state.yaml), AND
     - its work order exists (eos/specs/<id>.spec.md, spec_status: WRITTEN).
   Check readiness: `pnpm eos:compile` then read eos/state/project.health.yaml
   (work_order_coverage_pct) and eos/state/project.waves.yaml (order).
3. Paste the TEMPLATE below, with {SPEC} replaced by the full contents of the
   work-order file. Give the model repo access.
4. When it opens a PR, YOU (architect) review it. Do not auto-merge.
